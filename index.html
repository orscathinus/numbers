Special Exhibit 001 · After 1–4

π breaks the sequence.

Every four regular natural-number exhibits, the museum opens a door to a number of a different kind. The first special exhibit sits between 3 and 4, but it is neither: π is irrational, transcendental, and defined by the geometry of every Euclidean circle.

π
Special Object 001 · Pi

The circle without end.

π≈3.1415926535… is the ratio of circumference to diameter. From that simple definition it expands into radians, trigonometry, infinite series, probability, Gaussian statistics, Fourier analysis, physics, and transcendence.
