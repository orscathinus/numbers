Fact-checking convention notes

“Natural number” is convention-dependent: some authors include 0, others begin at 1. The exhibition therefore prefers “first positive integer” when making a convention-independent statement. Historical claims about glyph evolution are phrased as broad lineage rather than a simplistic one-shape-to-one-shape chain.

Method

Museum standard

Mathematical claims should be definition-aware. Historical claims should distinguish evidence from attractive origin stories. Future number exhibits should follow the same rule.
